About the Domus 26 West

The Domus 26 West is a thoughtfully planned standalone residential and commercial tower in Pushpak Node, Navi Mumbai. Designed by Atul Patel Architects, it offers well-proportioned 1 and 2 BHK homes alongside retail spaces, supported by modern amenities, a terrace garden, and strong build quality. Created for comfort and convenience, it reflects Domus’ approach to practical, enduring urban living.
